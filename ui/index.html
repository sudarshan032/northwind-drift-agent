Change Guardian

Before you patch a hospital server, see what it really breaks.

An AI agent that checks IT changes against what systems actually do.

Change Guardian is an AI agent. It compares what the hospital's IT records say with what its systems actually do, and warns you when a “safe” change isn't.

Try it yourself

Northwind Health is a fictional hospital system: 51 hospitals, 1,000 clinics and two data centres in seven western states. Every system, change, incident and user here is synthetic.

Hospital IT keeps a map. The map goes out of date.

Every hospital keeps a record of its systems and what depends on what, called a . People approving changes trust it. But systems change every week, and nobody updates the record. A patch that looks harmless on the map can break something nobody knew was connected.

What the agent does

  1. Reads the records

    The : every system and the dependencies people wrote down.

  2. Checks the evidence

    10 discovery, monitoring and log sources show what systems actually do.

  3. Walks the graph

    Follows every dependency, many steps deep, in FalkorDB.

  4. Decides and acts

    Risk, who to warn, a note on the change, and a fix for a person to approve.

Following dependencies is what graphs are built for.

In a 3.5-million-node CMDB, finding the 105,713 components that depend on one storage array takes 38 ms inside FalkorDB and 357 ms inside a tuned PostgreSQL. PostgreSQL is faster at the final reporting step, so for big changes the fastest design uses both.
